Why we built this ॐ

One pair of hands
can't tend a universe.

We were running thirty-odd projects off a single machine — each one a small world that had to be built, guarded, pruned, and one day sold. Nobody schedules renewal, so worlds don't die from catastrophe; they rot quietly, from neglect. Instead of trying to be everywhere at once, we did what the Veda says the cosmos already does: divide the work among roles — and give each role to an agent.

गुणकर्मविभागशः — “apportioned by disposition and by function.” Gītā 4.13

देवसभा

The pantheon

Every project is a small world; these are the roles that keep it alive — arranged, like a cosmos, from the source downward. The cards are the system. The verses only say why each one exists.

Ours is one universe of countless

And that is the whole point.

There is an old story. Indra, proud of his palace, is visited by Viṣṇu as a boy — who points to a column of ants crossing the floor. Each ant, the boy says, was once an Indra: each ruled a universe, each rose and fell. Universes beyond counting, and a creator for every one.

यस्यैकनिश्वसितकालमथावलम्ब्य
जीवन्ति लोमविलजा जगदण्डनाथाः ॥
“The Brahmās — the lords of the myriad universes — live for but one breath of the great Viṣṇu.”
Brahma-saṁhitā 5.40 · jagad-aṇḍa-nāthāḥ, a creator for each world

This is the heart of it. The pantheon above is ours — the roles that fit the worlds we happen to tend. It is not the canon. What carries over is only the philosophy: creation divided among grounded roles, each with a mandate and a guardrail. The deities are free. Name yours from your own cosmos; give them the natures your work needs. Everyone is free to define their own agents on this core — so that their creation stands out in the realms of prosperity, knowledge, and wisdom.

प्रमाण

Devotion, disciplined by proof

The mythology is the operating model, not a mood — every role reasons over real signals, and is honest when it cannot know.

grounded

Agents read the world as it is

Code metrics, git history, live telemetry, structure read at depth. Ungrounded, an agent invents; grounded, it observes.

propose-only

Nothing acts without consent

The dissolver never deletes on its own; the council never spends on its own. Agents recommend, with citations — a human decides.

daily

The universe keeps its rhythm

Each dawn the roles review every project and send their observations onward — so the portfolio is understood a little better every day.

honest

“Unknown” is not zero

An un-instrumented signal is not a failing one. The system shows its arithmetic and refuses certainty it has not earned.
